What does Dork Pro do?

Dork Pro builds Google search operator queries — "Google dorks" — either from a library of 30+ ready-made SEO presets (link building, technical audits, security/file discovery, social mentions, content ideas) or from a custom builder where you click individual operators like site:, intitle:, and filetype: to construct your own query from scratch.

Pick a preset, fill in your keyword or domain, and the query updates live in the preview box — copy it or click Search to run it directly on Google. Switch to Custom Builder mode for full manual control when a preset doesn't quite fit what you're trying to find.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dork Pro free to use?

Yes, completely free with no account or usage limit.

What is a Google dork?

It's a search query built from Google's advanced search operators (like site:, filetype:, or intitle:) to find specific types of content — competitor backlinks, unindexed subdomains, exposed files, guest post opportunities, and more — that a plain keyword search wouldn't surface.

Is using Google dorks against the rules?

The operators themselves are standard, publicly documented Google search features — using them isn't against Google's terms. That said, some presets here (like finding exposed config or SQL files) surface content site owners likely didn't intend to be public; treat anything you find responsibly and don't access or use data you're not authorised to.

Does it search on my behalf automatically?

No — it only builds the query. Clicking Search opens a new tab and runs it on Google yourself; nothing happens automatically in the background.

What's the difference between Preset Library and Custom Builder mode?

Preset Library gives you ready-made query templates for common SEO tasks — just fill in a keyword or domain. Custom Builder starts from a blank query and lets you click individual operators to construct exactly the query you need.
